Account Deletion

Parking Panic 3D – Truth Interactive

How to Request Account Deletion

Players of Parking Panic 3D can request deletion of their game account and associated personal data by contacting Truth Interactive using the email address below.

Submit a Deletion Request

Send an email with the following details:

  • Subject: Parking Panic 3D – Account Deletion Request
  • Your in-game Player ID, if available
  • Your account type (Guest or Google Play Games)

Email us to request account deletion

What Data Will Be Deleted?

After verifying your request, we will delete or arrange deletion of data associated with your game account, including:

  • Game account identifiers and associated profile data.
  • Cloud-saved game progress, scores and achievements.
  • Associated gameplay data stored by our services.

Data That May Be Retained

Limited purchase transaction records may be retained where required for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes. Such records will be retained only for the period required by applicable law or the relevant legal obligation and will not be used to restore your deleted game account.

Google Play may independently retain purchase and account records under its own policies.

Processing of Requests

We may contact you to verify ownership of the account. Once verified, eligible deletion requests will be processed within 30 days. If certain information must legally be retained, it will be restricted to the applicable retention purpose.

Guest Accounts

Guest game data may be stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data may remove locally stored progress. Contact us if you need help with an account deletion request.
